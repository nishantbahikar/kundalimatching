About kundali matching

What is Guna Milan?

Ashtakoot Guna Milan is the Vedic astrology method of kundali matching that evaluates 8 compatibility factors (koots) and assigns a score out of 36. A score of 18+ is considered acceptable for marriage.

The 8 Ashtakoots

Varna (1), Vashya (2), Tara (3), Yoni (4), Graha Maitri (5), Gana (6), Bhakoot (7), Nadi (8). Nadi has the highest weight and is the most critical compatibility factor.

Mangal & Nadi dosha

Mangal dosha is checked separately from Guna Milan. Nadi dosha (same nadi for both) scores 0/8 and is considered serious. Both doshas have traditional cancellation rules.
